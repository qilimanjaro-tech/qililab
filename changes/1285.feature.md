Added `qp.qblox.measure_herald(bus, waveform, weights, max_attempts=10, trigger_address=1)` to herald on the ground state. Each shot measures the bus until the qubit is in the ground state, up to `max_attempts` times, then every Qblox bus waits for the others and the rest of the QProgram runs. If no attempt finds the ground state, the shot runs unheralded.

```python
qp = QProgram()
frequency = qp.variable(label="frequency", domain=Domain.Frequency)
with qp.average(shots=1000):
    with qp.for_loop(variable=frequency, start=4e6, stop=5e6, step=0.1e6):  # 11 points
        # Measure until the qubit is in the ground state, at most 10 times
        qp.qblox.measure_herald(bus="readout_q0", waveform="Measure", weights="Weights")
        # Every Qblox bus starts again together here
        qp.set_frequency(bus="drive_q0", frequency=frequency)
        qp.play(bus="drive_q0", waveform="Drag")
        qp.sync()
        qp.measure(bus="readout_q0", waveform="Measure", weights="Weights")
        # At least 400 ns between this measurement and the next shot's herald
        qp.wait(bus="readout_q0", duration=400)

results = platform.execute_qprogram(qp, calibration=calibration)
herald, measure = results.results["readout_q0"]
herald.array.shape  # (2, 11, 10): I/Q, frequency points, attempts
measure.array.shape  # (2, 11)
```

- The herald returns one result on its bus, with the attempts as last axis: attempt `k` writes entry `k`, and the attempts skipped after the ground state was found stay NaN.
- A QProgram can have only one `measure_herald`, which can't be used together with `wait_trigger`, `if_trigger` or `qp.qblox.measure_reset()`. Running it in an `Experiment` raises a `NotImplementedError`: use `platform.execute_qprogram()`.
- Leave at least 400 ns on the bus between its last measurement and the herald, for example the previous shot's last `qp.measure()`: a trigger from that measurement can otherwise be counted as the herald's ground state.
- The trigger of a `measure_reset` measurement bus is now set to fire on the excited state at each run, so a previous herald on the same bus doesn't leave it inverted.
