Added `qp.qblox.measure_herald(bus, waveform, weights, max_attempts=50, trigger_address=1)` to herald on the ground state. Each shot measures the bus until the qubit is in the ground state, up to `max_attempts` times, then every Qblox bus waits for the others and the rest of the QProgram runs. If no attempt finds the ground state, the shot runs unheralded.

- The herald returns one result on its bus, with the attempts as last axis: attempt `k` writes entry `k`, and the attempts skipped after the ground state was found stay NaN.
- A QProgram can have only one `measure_herald`, which can't be used together with `wait_trigger`, `if_trigger` or `qp.qblox.measure_reset()`. Running it in an `Experiment` raises a `NotImplementedError`: use `platform.execute_qprogram()`.
- Leave at least 400 ns on the bus between its last measurement and the herald, for example the previous shot's last `qp.measure()`: a trigger from that measurement can otherwise be counted as the herald's ground state.
- The trigger of a `measure_reset` measurement bus is now set to fire on the excited state at each run, so a previous herald on the same bus doesn't leave it inverted.
