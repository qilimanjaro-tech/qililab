Fixed crosstalk compensation (`QProgram.with_crosstalk_qblox`) crashing on a flux-vs-flux sweep whose two flux loops have different lengths, with `ValueError: operands could not be broadcast together with shapes (39,) (38,)`. The compensation eagerly combined each bus's full sweep array element-wise, which is undefined when the buses are swept by independent loops of different sizes. Offset and gain sweeps are now accumulated without that eager cross product (the per-variable compensation is applied via `get_decomposed_vector`), while `play` waveforms stay strict and still raise on a genuine length mismatch.
