The number of points of a `for_loop` is now computed in one place, `for_loop_iterations` in `qililab.qprogram.blocks`, instead of five copies across the Qblox, QDAC-II and Quantum Machines compilers, crosstalk compensation and `ExperimentExecutor`. The private `_calculate_iterations` methods of `QbloxCompiler`, `QdacCompiler` and `QuantumMachinesCompiler` are removed.
