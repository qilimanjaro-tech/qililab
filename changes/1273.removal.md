The move of `QProgram` onto the `qprogram` libraries changes or removes, without a deprecation period unless stated:

- `qp.variable(label, Domain.X, type)` is now `qp.variable(id, label=None, units=None)`, with an id matching `[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*`. `ql.Domain`, `IntVariable`, `FloatVariable` and `qililab.core` are removed.
- `qp.for_loop()` and `qp.parallel(loops=[ForLoop(...), ...])` still work but are deprecated (`FutureWarning`) and will be removed in a future release. `for_loop` keeps its number of points, rounded down when `step` does not divide `stop - start`, spread from `start` to `stop`; `from_range` rounds the number of points to the nearest. `qp.parallel` builds the same block as sweeps combined with `|`, so its loops must now have the same number of points: loops of different lengths raise a `qprogram.ValidationError` instead of running as many points as the shortest.
- `qp.loop(variable, values)` is removed from `QProgram` (it only ever worked on QDAC buses): set up the QDAC before the loop with `qp.qdac.play(..., repetitions=N)`. `Experiment.loop()` is unchanged.
- `qp.wait_trigger()` and `qp.set_trigger()` are removed: use `qp.qblox.wait_trigger()` / `qp.qblox.set_trigger()` on Qblox buses, `qp.qdac.wait_trigger(bus, port)` / `qp.qdac.set_trigger(bus, duration, position, outputs)` on QDAC buses. A QDAC bus also needs `qp.qdac.play()` and `qp.qdac.set_offset(bus, offset)`: `play()` and `set_offset()` on a QDAC bus raise a `NotImplementedError`. QDAC operations run before the loops, so a QDAC operation inside a loop is rejected. `qp.qdac.play()` takes its defaults from qprogram-qdac, and `repetitions` defaults to 1 instead of a count derived from the loops compiled before the play. A QDAC `set_trigger` needs `outputs`, and a QDAC `wait_trigger` a `port`: the QDAC's internal triggers are no longer set up. A QDAC `play()` with an `IQPair`, of which only `I` was played, now raises a `qprogram.ValidationError`. So does a `qp.qdac.play()` delay outside 0 to 3600 s or a `qp.qdac.set_trigger()` duration outside 1 µs to 3600 s, the QDAC-II's ranges, which used to reach the instrument unchecked.
- `save_adc` is removed from `measure()`, `qp.qblox.acquire()` and `qp.qblox.measure_reset()`: use `qp.qblox.scope(bus)`. The runcard fields `scope_acquire_trigger_mode`, `scope_hardware_averaging` and `scope_store_enabled` are deprecated: they raise a `FutureWarning`, their value is ignored, and they will be removed in a future release. Their `Parameter` members (`Parameter.SCOPE_ACQUIRE_TRIGGER_MODE`, `Parameter.SCOPE_HARDWARE_AVERAGING`, `Parameter.SCOPE_STORE_ENABLED`) are removed, as `Parameter.INTEGRATION_LENGTH` was.
- `if_trigger()` used together with a `wait_trigger` or a `measure_reset` raises its `NotImplementedError` when the program is compiled, no longer when it is built, with the same message whichever comes first.
- A loop with a zero step raises a `qprogram.ValidationError` when it is built, no longer a `ValueError` when it is compiled.
- `QProgramResults`, `MeasurementResult`, `QbloxMeasurementResult` and `qililab.result.qprogram` are removed: results are a `qprogram.QProgramResult`. The raw Qblox acquisitions (`raw_measurement_data`) are not exposed.
- `AcquisitionData.save_adc` is removed; `AcquisitionData` now records the measurements writing into the acquisition (`measurements`, `fields`) and the loops around it (`loops`). `QbloxQRM.acquire_qprogram_results()` and `Bus.acquire_qprogram_results()` return the raw acquisitions and take `scope=`.
- `QProgram.with_resolved_weight_duration()` is removed: `qp.qblox.weight_duration` is now read from the program's measurements, keyed by the buses after the bus mapping. A measurement whose weights are not in the calibration raises when the program is compiled, no longer when the weight duration is read.
- `str(qprogram)` returns the `.qp` text of the program.
- `qililab.waveforms` is removed: the waveforms are qprogram's, still importable from `qililab` (`ql.Square`, `ql.IQDrag`, ..., and now `ql.IQWaveform`). `IQPair.DRAG`, deprecated since 0.32.0, is removed: use `IQDrag`. The shapes are the same, with these differences: `Arbitrary.envelope(resolution)` ignores the resolution and returns the samples as they are; an `IQPair` whose waveforms have different durations raises a `qprogram.ValidationError` instead of a `ValueError`; `Square` and `Ramp` sampled at a resolution that doesn't divide their duration have one sample less (`Square(1.0, 7).envelope(2)` has 3 samples, not 4); a `Square` with an integer amplitude has integer samples; and `get_duration()` is an integer (`Square(1.0, 10.7)` lasts 10 ns). `qililab.qprogram.blocks` and `qililab.qprogram.operations` only hold what qililab adds (`InfiniteLoop`, `IfTrigger`, formerly `Conditional`, `MeasureReset`, `QbloxPlay`, the Experiment operations): the others are in `qprogram.blocks`, `qprogram.operations`, `qprogram_qblox` and `qprogram_qdac`.
- `Experiment` is now a `qprogram.QProgram`: `experiment.variable(id)` replaces `variable(label, domain, type)`.
- Quantum Machines is no longer supported: the `quantum-machines` extra, `qililab.extra`, `QuantumMachinesCompiler`, `qp.quantum_machines`, and the Quantum Machines instrument and controller are removed. A runcard with a Quantum Machines cluster no longer loads.
- The modules of `qililab.qprogram` are grouped into subpackages: `qblox_compiler`, `qdac_compiler` and `prepass` move to `qililab.qprogram.compilers`, `crosstalk_matrix`, `flux_vector` and `utils_crosstalk` to `qililab.qprogram.crosstalk`, and `experiment` and `experiment_executor` to `qililab.qprogram.experiment`. For example, `qililab.qprogram.qblox_compiler` is now `qililab.qprogram.compilers.qblox_compiler`. The names exported by `qililab` and `qililab.qprogram` are unchanged.
