The move of `QProgram` onto the `qprogram` libraries changes or removes, without a deprecation period unless stated (programs that ran wrongly without a warning and now raise are listed with the bug fixes):

- `qp.variable(label, Domain.X, type)` is now `qp.variable(id, label=None, units=None)`, with an id matching `[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*`. A plus sign in front of a variable (`+x`) is refused like other unsupported arithmetic: write `x`. `ql.Domain`, `IntVariable`, `FloatVariable` and `qililab.core` are removed. Variables are told apart by id, no longer by UUID: `insert_block()` of a block with a loop over a variable whose id a loop around it already sweeps raises `VariableAllocated`.
- `qp.for_loop()` still works but is deprecated (`FutureWarning`) and will be removed in a future release. It sweeps the same points as before, as a `qp.sweep(variable).from_linspace(start, stop, num)` whose number of points `num` is rounded down when `step` does not divide `stop - start`, and a `.qp` file shows it as a `Linspace`. qprogram's `qp.sweep(variable).from_range(start, stop, step)`, which keeps the step and rounds the number of points to the nearest, is refused (`qprogram.ValidationError`): qililab's loops were always linspaces.
- `qp.parallel(loops=[...])`, `Experiment.parallel(loops=[...])` and `Loop` are removed: combine sweeps with `|`, as in `qp.sweep(a).from_linspace(...) | qp.sweep(b).from_linspace(...)`, or, in an `Experiment`, `experiment.sweep(a).from_values(...) | experiment.sweep(b).from_values(...)`. The sweeps must have the same number of points; where `qp.parallel` ran as many points as the shortest loop, `|` raises a `qprogram.ValidationError`. A QProgram saved as YAML with such a `qp.parallel` no longer loads: `ql.deserialize()` raises a `DeserializationError`.
- `qp.loop(variable, values)` is removed from `QProgram` (it only ever worked on QDAC buses): set up the QDAC before the loop with `qp.qdac.play(..., repetitions=N)`. `Experiment.loop()` is unchanged.
- `qp.wait_trigger()` and `qp.set_trigger()` are removed: use `qp.qblox.wait_trigger()` / `qp.qblox.set_trigger()` on Qblox buses, `qp.qdac.wait_trigger(bus, port)` / `qp.qdac.set_trigger(bus, duration, outputs, position)` on QDAC buses, `outputs` being one port or a list of them as before. A QDAC bus also needs `qp.qdac.play()` and `qp.qdac.set_offset(bus, offset)`: `play()` and `set_offset()` on a QDAC bus raise a `NotImplementedError`. `QProgram.draw()` draws the Qblox buses only: a bus holding QDAC operations is left out. `qp.qdac.play()` takes its defaults from qprogram-qdac; `repetitions` is still worked out from the loops when it isn't given. A QDAC `set_trigger` needs `outputs`, and a QDAC `wait_trigger` a `port`: the QDAC's internal triggers are no longer set up.
- `save_adc` is removed from `measure()`, `qp.qblox.acquire()` and `qp.qblox.measure_reset()`: use `qp.qblox.scope(bus)`. The runcard fields `scope_acquire_trigger_mode`, `scope_hardware_averaging` and `scope_store_enabled` are deprecated: they raise a `FutureWarning`, their value is ignored, and they will be removed in a future release. Their `Parameter` members (`Parameter.SCOPE_ACQUIRE_TRIGGER_MODE`, `Parameter.SCOPE_HARDWARE_AVERAGING`, `Parameter.SCOPE_STORE_ENABLED`) are removed, as `Parameter.INTEGRATION_LENGTH` was.
- `if_trigger()` used together with a `wait_trigger` or a `measure_reset` raises its `NotImplementedError` when the program is compiled, no longer when it is built, with the same message whichever comes first. Its `trigger_padding_ns` and `expected_wait_time_ns` are stored as integers: a float loses its fraction when it is given, no longer when the waits are computed, so a `trigger_padding_ns` with a fraction makes the wait that completes the trigger period 1 ns longer.
- `qp.for_loop()` with a zero step raises its `ValueError` when the loop is built, no longer when the program is compiled, and one whose step points away from `stop` raises a `qprogram.ValidationError` when it is built, where it raised a `ValueError` (`Iterations must be greater than 0.`) when compiled.
- `QProgramResults`, `MeasurementResult`, `QbloxMeasurementResult`, `ResultName.QBLOX_QPROGRAM_MEASUREMENT` and `qililab.result.qprogram` are removed: results are a `qprogram.QProgramResult`. The raw Qblox acquisitions (`raw_measurement_data`) are not exposed.
- `AcquisitionData.save_adc` is removed; `AcquisitionData` now records the measurements writing into the acquisition (`measurements`, `fields`) and the loops around it (`loops`). `QbloxQRM.acquire_qprogram_results()` and `Bus.acquire_qprogram_results()` return the raw acquisitions and take `scope=`.
- `QProgram.with_resolved_weight_duration()` is removed: `qp.qblox.weight_duration` is now read from the program's measurements, keyed by the buses after the bus mapping. A measurement whose weights are not in the calibration raises when the program is compiled, no longer when the weight duration is read.
- `str(qprogram)` returns the `.qp` text of the program.
- `qililab.waveforms` is removed: the waveforms are qprogram's, still importable from `qililab` (`ql.Square`, `ql.IQDrag`, ..., and now `ql.IQWaveform`). `IQPair.DRAG`, deprecated since 0.32.0, is removed: use `IQDrag`. The shapes are the same, with these differences: `Arbitrary.envelope(resolution)` ignores the resolution and returns the samples as they are; an `IQPair` whose waveforms have different durations raises a `qprogram.ValidationError` instead of a `ValueError`; `Square` and `Ramp` sampled at a resolution that doesn't divide their duration have one sample less (`Square(1.0, 7).envelope(2)` has 3 samples, not 4); a `Square` with an integer amplitude has integer samples; and `get_duration()` is an integer (`Square(1.0, 10.7)` lasts 10 ns). `qililab.qprogram.blocks` and `qililab.qprogram.operations` only hold what qililab adds (`InfiniteLoop`, `IfTrigger`, formerly `Conditional`, `MeasureReset`, `QbloxPlay`): the others are in `qprogram.blocks`, `qprogram.operations`, `qprogram_qblox` and `qprogram_qdac`.
- `Experiment` is now a `qprogram.QProgram`: `experiment.variable(id)` replaces `variable(label, domain, type)`.
- Quantum Machines is no longer supported: the `quantum-machines` extra, `qililab.extra`, `QuantumMachinesCompiler`, `qp.quantum_machines`, and the Quantum Machines instrument and controller are removed. A runcard with a Quantum Machines cluster no longer loads.
- The modules of `qililab.qprogram` are grouped into subpackages: `qblox_compiler`, `qdac_compiler` and `prepass` move to `qililab.qprogram.compilers`, `crosstalk_matrix`, `flux_vector` and `utils_crosstalk` to `qililab.qprogram.crosstalk`, and `experiment` and `experiment_executor` to `qililab.qprogram.experiment`, with the Experiment operations (`ExecuteQProgram`, `GetParameter`, `SetCrosstalk`, `SetParameter`) in `qililab.qprogram.experiment.operations`. For example, `qililab.qprogram.qblox_compiler` is now `qililab.qprogram.compilers.qblox_compiler`. The names exported by `qililab` and `qililab.qprogram` are unchanged.
