A numpy number on the left of `+` or `-` with a QProgram variable no longer drops the variable silently. With the former variables, `np.int64(289) - time` gave the number 289 and compiled to a fixed `wait 289`, where `289 - time` gave the expression; both now give the expression. Programs written that way, such as a wait computed from `np.max(...)` of a sweep minus the swept variable, now run the timing they were written for.
