Added `flux_offsets` to the `Measurement` database table, saved automatically by `StreamArray` when a calibration with a crosstalk matrix is available. The stored flux vector is the sum of the platform's `flux_vector` and the flux defined by the QProgram's constant `set_offset` calls on crosstalk buses. Only buses offset exactly once by a scalar value are taken from the QProgram; swept offsets and buses offset more than once are ignored.
