A `for_loop` with a single point (`start == stop`, or a step larger than the range) no longer crashes the Qblox compiler with a `ZeroDivisionError`. It runs one iteration at `start`, with no `add`/`sub` in the Q1ASM. qililab now requires qpysequence 0.12.0 or later, the first version whose `IterativeLoop` accepts a step of 0.
