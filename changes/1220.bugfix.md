Fixed QDAC channels that play separate DC lists starting a command-latency apart instead of together. When crosstalk compensation spreads a single `qdac.play` across several channels of the same QDAC (e.g. a flux pulse on `flux1` compensated on `flux2`), each channel received its own DC list. `QDevilQDac2.start()` launched every list that was not armed on a trigger with an independent, sequential SCPI `start()` call, so the channels began microseconds apart and the compensated flux pulse was distorted.

`QdacCompiler` now synchronizes these channels onto a common hardware trigger. After compilation, `_synchronize_qdac_channels` elects one channel per QDAC as the master: it keeps starting on `start()` and emits an internal trigger at its list start, while every other list-playing channel on that QDAC is armed (`set_in_internal_trigger`) to start on that trigger, so they all begin together. When a start-marker trigger already exists on one of these channels (e.g. the one created by `set_trigger(position="start")` to drive the Qblox), it is reused as the synchronization source, so a single trigger both drives external hardware and aligns the QDAC channels; otherwise a dedicated internal trigger is allocated on the master. QDACs playing a single list are left unchanged.
