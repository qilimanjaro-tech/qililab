Fixed `wait_trigger` unconditionally emitting a `wait_sync` instruction on every Qblox bus after the `wait_trigger`, even when there was only one bus and nothing to synchronize with. `QbloxCompiler._handle_wait_trigger` no longer emits a separate `wait_sync`; instead, every Qblox bus in the QProgram now receives its own `wait_trigger` of the same requested duration, which keeps them synchronized without the extra instruction.
