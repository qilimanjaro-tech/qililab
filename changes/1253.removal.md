Removed `QProgram.qblox.latch_enabled` and `QProgram.qblox.trigger_network_required`, without a deprecation period. The Qblox compiler now derives both from the `qp.qblox.measure_reset()` operations of the QProgram, and the buses that send on the trigger network, with their trigger address, are in `QbloxCompilationOutput.trigger_network_required`.
