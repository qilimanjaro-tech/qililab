Removed `QProgram.qblox.latch_enabled` and `QProgram.qblox.trigger_network_required`, without a deprecation period. The Qblox compiler now derives both from the `qp.qblox.measure_reset()` operations of the QProgram, and the buses that send on the trigger network, with their trigger address, are in `QbloxCompilationOutput.trigger_network_required`.

The `qprogram` of `QbloxCompilationOutput` and of `QdacCompilationOutput` now only holds the operations of its instrument. Code that read another instrument's operations from it must read them from the QProgram given to `compile()` instead, through `QProgram.with_bus_mapping()` and `QProgram.with_calibration()` if it needs the bus mapping or the calibration applied.
