Added `qp.qblox.measure_herald(bus, waveform, weights, max_attempts=20)` to herald on the ground state. Each shot measures the bus until the qubit is in the ground state, up to `max_attempts` times, then every Qblox bus waits for the others and the rest of the QProgram runs. If no attempt finds the ground state, the shot runs unheralded. Each attempt's result comes back to the bus's sequencer over LINQ feedback, so it stops measuring as soon as the qubit is in the ground state: this needs Qblox cluster firmware 2.x.

```python
qp = QProgram()
frequency = qp.variable(label="frequency", domain=Domain.Frequency)
with qp.average(shots=1000):
    with qp.for_loop(variable=frequency, start=4e6, stop=5e6, step=0.1e6):  # 11 points
        # Measure until the qubit is in the ground state, at most 20 times
        qp.qblox.measure_herald(bus="readout_q0", waveform="Measure", weights="Weights")
        # Every Qblox bus starts again together here
        qp.set_frequency(bus="drive_q0", frequency=frequency)
        qp.play(bus="drive_q0", waveform="Drag")
        qp.sync()
        qp.measure(bus="readout_q0", waveform="Measure", weights="Weights")
        # At least 50 ns before the next herald
        qp.wait(bus="readout_q0", duration=50)

results = platform.execute_qprogram(qp, calibration=calibration)
herald, measure = results.results["readout_q0"]
herald.array.shape  # (2, 11, 20): I/Q, frequency points, attempts
measure.array.shape  # (2, 11)
```

- The herald returns one result on its bus, with the attempts as last axis: attempt `k` writes entry `k`, and the attempts after the one that found the ground state don't run, so their entries stay NaN.
- On its bus, the herald must come right after a `qp.wait` of at least 50 ns, at the end of the loop when the herald comes first in it: a measurement ending closer to the herald can be read as its first attempt, or the sequencer can hang. Compiling raises otherwise.
- A QProgram can have only one `measure_herald`, which can't be used together with `wait_trigger`, `if_trigger` or `qp.qblox.measure_reset()`. Running it in an `Experiment` raises a `NotImplementedError`: use `platform.execute_qprogram()`.
